# Enquiry contacts for the 76 respite centres

Researched 2026-09 from each provider's public website + web search. **Not
machine-checked and not wired into the app** — this is a reference sheet for a
caregiver (or a future "send" feature) deciding where an enquiry should go.

Grouped by operator (several branches share one channel). The `#` column is the
record's position in [`services.json`](services.json).

**Legend** — `email`: a published general/enquiry address · `form`: the main
channel is a web contact form · `phone`: no email found, call instead · `AIC`:
admission is via the Agency for Integrated Care hotline **1800-650-6060** (true
for every subsidised nursing-home respite record regardless of what's below).

## Where a form is the only option

These have **no published enquiry email**; the operator's web contact form (or
a phone call) is the way in:

- **Homage** — `homage.sg/contact-us` ("Speak to our Care Advisors")
- **All Saints Home** — `allsaintshome.org.sg/contact-us` (has a centre picker + file upload)
- **Bukit Batok Care Home** — via Vanguard Healthcare's form, or AIC
- **Irene / Serene Nursing Home** — form on `elderlynursing.com`
- **Man Fut Tong Nursing Home** — form on `mft.org.sg`, or call for a visit slot
- **Tampines Care Home** — no site found; enquire through AIC 1800-650-6060

## Caveat

Every subsidised nursing-home respite stay (records 14–76) is **admitted
through AIC**, not by contacting the home directly — the emails above are for
questions/tours, not booking. Figures and addresses were current at time of
research; re-verify before use.
